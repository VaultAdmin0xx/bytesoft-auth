Account and login

  • Forgot your password? Passwords can't be recovered, only reset by the administrator.
  • Logged out unexpectedly? Sessions last 2 hours, so just log in again.
  • Username already taken? Choose a different one when you sign up.
📋

Dashboard

  • Something looks out of date? Refresh the page.
  • Projects, meetings and leave requests are saved in this browser only.
  • Can't see your data on another device? That is expected. Use the same browser.
🛠️

Connection problems

  • If the status above says the server is unreachable, wait a moment and refresh.
  • Byte and Public Chat need the server to be running.
  • Voice input works best in Chrome, over a secure (https) connection.
