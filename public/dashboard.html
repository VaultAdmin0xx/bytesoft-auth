About ByteSoft

ByteSoft is a lightweight workspace dashboard for individuals and small teams. It brings your projects, meeting schedule, personal profile and leave requests together in one place, so you don't need to jump between separate tools to see what's going on.

Ask Byte, the built-in assistant, to summarize your dashboard, add or edit projects and meetings, open your profile, or apply for leave — by typing or speaking into the mic. Your dashboard data is kept in this browser (and synced to your account if a backend is connected), so it's ready whenever you log back in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a project?
Click the + button in Projects, enter the project details and save. You can edit or delete it later.
How do I add or change a meeting?
Use the + button beside Meeting Schedule. Each meeting also has an edit button and a delete button.
How do I edit my profile?
Click your name or profile card to open the full profile page, then edit the available fields.
What can Byte do?
Byte can inspect the current dashboard, summarize projects and meetings, open your profile, and perform supported add/edit/delete actions through chat.
Where are my browser edits stored?
This version stores dashboard edits in localStorage so the interface remains usable without a database. Your backend can be connected through the API hooks in the JavaScript.
